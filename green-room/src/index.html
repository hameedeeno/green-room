Green Room

A breath coach for people who talk for a living

Green Room learns how you breathe when you are quiet, then counts how long you talk without stopping. When the stretch gets long, it dims the screen and shows you one word.

  • Camera only. No microphone is used at any point.
  • Silent first. It spends the first half minute learning your resting breathing, and says nothing.
  • Nothing is recorded. No video, no audio, no vitals are written to disk by this app.
  • Processing is local. Signals are computed on this machine by the Presage SDK. The only network call is the SDK's API key check.
  • SDK telemetry is off. This app constructs the SDK with telemetry disabled.
  • It is wellness and performance feedback, not a medical device. Nothing here diagnoses or treats anything.
